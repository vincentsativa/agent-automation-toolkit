# Pinterest Niche Market Research Reference

Key statistics for sizing and validating Pinterest niche opportunities.

## Platform Scale (2025-2026)

- **MAUs:** 578M (2025) → 619M (2025 year-end, +12% YoY)
- **Revenue:** $3.6B (2024) → $4.2B (2025, +16% YoY) → $4.75B projected (2026)
- **Ad reach:** ~340M users reachable by ads (Jan 2025)
- **Monthly web visits:** ~1.1-1.3B
- **Rank:** ~15th most used social platform worldwide by MAU
- **Daily time spent:** ~14.2 min/user (lowest among major social — Facebook 33m, Twitter 31m, Insta 29m)

## Top Content Categories (by Pin volume)

1. **Home décor** — 23B+ Pins, 682M boards (largest category)
2. **Food & recipes** — 18B+ Pins
3. **Fashion** — 13B+ Pins
4. **DIY & crafts** — High engagement, sub-niches like Cricut outperform
5. **Travel** — Nature getaways +72%, Kilimanjaro +221%
6. **Wellness** — "Nervous system regulation" +70%, "art journal therapy" +3,755%
7. **Sustainable living** — +69% since 2018

AI/Tech is NOT a top Pin-volume category — it's an underserved/emerging niche.

## Tech Audience Signal (Referral Data)

Desktop referral traffic breakdown to Pinterest.com:
- Programming & Developer Software: **25.3%** (largest single category)
- Search Engines: 24.5%
- AI Chatbots & Tools: **11.9%**
- Computers/Electronics/Tech: 2.6%
- Graphics/Multimedia/Web Design: 2.5%

**Combined tech-oriented categories: 42.3%** of all desktop referral traffic. Tech-savvy people are heavily on Pinterest even if they don't create AI pins.

## Demographic Profile

- **Gen Z:** 42% of global MAUs — fastest growing segment, saves more pins than any other generation
- **Women:** ~70% of users (historically dominant but Gen Z is more balanced)
- **U.S. users:** ~96-97M (largest national audience)
- **Unbranded searches:** 96%+ — users search for "ideas" and "inspiration" not products

## Growth Signals for Niche Validation

| Signal | Where to Check | What It Means |
|--------|---------------|---------------|
| Trends "Spotlight" | trends.pinterest.com | Pin-save MoM growth (e.g., "Travel Aesthetic Destinations +10,001%") |
| Search Trends | trends.pinterest.com/trends/searches | Keyword-level trending searches from Pin save data |
| Pinterest Predicts | business.pinterest.com/pinterest-predicts/ | 80% accurate annual forecast — themed trend names signal category direction |
| Autocomplete | Pinterest search bar | Real user search terms with platform-validated volume |
| Search result density | Direct search result page | High supply = competitive, low/sparse = underserved opportunity |
| Sponsored content | Search results page | Advertisers spend where purchase intent exists |
| Third-party stats | techrt.com, omnicore, doofinder | Category rankings, referral traffic, ad revenue trends |

## Pinterest as AI Company

Pinterest is heavily investing in AI:
- Launched AI-powered shopping assistant (2025)
- Acquired TVScientific (CTV ad tech)
- Cut 15% workforce to reallocate resources to AI development
- AI auto-collages: 2x the save rate of standard product pins
- Performance+ ad campaigns: 24% higher conversions, up to 52% CPA reduction
- AI-driven discovery is the core differentiation from entertainment-first platforms

## Shopping & Commerce Stats (for brand/advertising analysis)

- Pinterest shoppers spend **2x more** per month than on other platforms
- 85% of weekly Pinners have purchased based on a brand Pin
- **32% higher ROAS** than other digital platforms (Nielsen)
- Shopping ads: **3x higher conversion and sales lift** vs other platforms
- 76% of users open to **trying new brands** (higher than U.S. average)
- 83% have purchased based on branded content seen on Pinterest
