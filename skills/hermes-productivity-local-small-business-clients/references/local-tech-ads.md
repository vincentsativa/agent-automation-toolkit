# Local Tech Help Ads

Two ads, two platforms. Same pitch, different tone.
Craigslist = direct and transactional. Nextdoor = warm and neighborly.
Both target the same buyer: non-tech-savvy small business owners who pay cash for a same-day fix.

## AD 1: CRAIGSLIST (under "computer services")

**Title:** Local Tech Help - Same Day, Flat Fee, No Contracts

**Body:**

Local tech help. I fix the stuff that's driving you crazy.

- Email not working? Fixed today.
- Website broken or outdated? Fixed today.
- Invoicing, spreadsheets, or bookkeeping a mess? Fixed today.
- Computer slow, crashing, or acting up? Fixed today.

No jargon. No contracts. No hourly meter running while you watch.

You tell me the problem in plain English. I fix it. You pay when it works.

Flat fee, cash or Venmo. Same-day service in the Ronkonkoma area.

Call or text [YOUR NUMBER].

> **CRITICAL:** Do NOT put the phone number in the Craigslist ad body. Craigslist anonymizes email replies by default and flags ads with numbers. Let buyers reply through the relay, then exchange numbers in the reply. Remove the "Call or text" line.

## AD 2: NEXTDOOR (recommendations section)

**Title:** Local tech help for small business owners

**Body:**

Hi neighbors. I'm a local tech guy in Ronkonkoma, and I help small business owners fix the computer problems that eat up their day.

If your email is acting up, your website is broken or outdated, your invoicing is a mess, or your computer is just slow and frustrating, I can fix it. Usually same day.

I keep it simple: you tell me the problem in plain English, I fix it, and you pay a flat fee when it works. No contracts, no hourly meter, no tech talk.

I've helped a few local shops already and they've been happy. Happy to do the same for you.

If that sounds useful, send me a message here or text [YOUR NUMBER].

> Nextdoor is fine with a phone number.

## Quick notes
- Replace [YOUR NUMBER] with real contact before posting.
- Craigslist: post in local area's "computer services" section. Renew the ad every 48 hours to keep it near the top.
- Nextdoor: post in "recommendations" section, and reply directly to anyone asking for tech help.
- Filter hard: meet in person or at their business, never take wire transfers or "overpayment" checks, flat fee paid on completion.
- The play: build the script once, sell the same fix to ten local businesses. Pure margin after the first one.
