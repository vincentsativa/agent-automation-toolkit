# AI BI Agent: Step-by-Step Business Intelligence Guide (Full Text)

Source: stepbystep.pdf provided by Vinny (from Joe)

## Phase 1: Competitor Site Registration and Partner Identification

1. **Navigate and Input Data**: Go to the competitor's registration site. Input Personally Identifiable Information (PII), which includes an address, a date of birth (e.g., 0429), an email address, and a random phone number.
2. **Locate Partner Link**: Look for a hyperlink on the registration page that uses language like "marketing partners" or "partners".
3. **Extract Partner DBAs**: If the partner link is found, navigate to that page. Copy all listed Doing Business As (DBAs) names from the marketing partners page and record them in the BI sheet. These names are potential future advertisers.
4. **Submit Registration**: After recording the initial DBAs, submit the registration page.

## Phase 2: Ad Creative and Offer Link Capture

1. **Maximize Ad Exposure**: Start answering the pre-qualifying questions presented after registration. Select all possible outcomes for these questions to ensure the maximum number of ads are served.
2. **Capture Ad Data (Per Ad)**: For every ad served, perform the following documentation steps:
   - **Creative**: Copy or screenshot the ad creative and place it into the BI flow (CVS/Google Sheet).
   - **Position**: Note the ad's position, as a higher position suggests a higher potential payout.
   - **Offer Link**: Copy the link to the offer/landing page and paste it into the BI sheet next to the corresponding ad creative.
3. **Document Ad-Specific DBAs**: Analyze the descriptive language at the bottom of the ad creative for listed company names (DBAs), such as National Health Advisor or Aragon Advertising LLC. Record these specific DBAs in the BI sheet, ideally alongside the question that prompted the ad.

## Phase 3: Advertiser Identification and Contact Strategy

1. **Identify Actual Advertiser**: Go through all collected linkouts (offers and qualifications pages). Examine supporting pages, such as privacy policies, to definitively identify the actual advertiser company (e.g., Globe Life Inc.).
2. **Locate Decision-Maker**: Use platforms like LinkedIn to find a decision-maker within the identified advertiser company to discuss driving traffic to their site.
