# Job Lead Screening (Vinny's constraints)

## When to use
Vinny forwards a list of job leads (usually 10-30 leads from a research agent) and asks which ones to apply to. He is the applicant. The screening must respect two hard constraints, one income and one legal, plus one hard gate on the application path: every lead must reach the employer's own form or a direct application email without him creating an account on a job board.

## The two constraints (do not screen without them)

### 1. Income: screen into HIS band, $1,000 to $2,500 a month gross
Vinny's operating band is $1,000 to $2,500 a month gross. That is the number to screen against. He stated it directly and it is a deliberate risk decision, not ignorance of the rules: he needs $1,000-$2,500 a month and he cannot take risks over the next ten months. Background he gave: four months before Sept 2026, SSA attributed his father's condo to him as an excess resource, suspended his wife's SSI, and terminated her Medicaid on two weeks notice. The margin for error is zero. Screen into the band.

**Above the band is not automatically out, and it is not one bucket.** There are two standing money paths, both his own decision, and they are separated by engagement shape rather than by pay level:

- **The band, $1,000 to $2,500 a month gross.** The default zone, and the majority of any list he receives.
- **$80,000 a year or more shaped as 1099, contract or freelance, with no background check, or from an employer that hires felons.** He will take that, and he accepts the coverage tradeoff at that level. This is a standing path, not a momentary override, so do not screen it out and do not argue him back down to the band. Name the tradeoff once and move on.
- **Salaried W-2 above the band** is the case that still needs the coverage math and a flag, because there the tradeoff is a decision he has not already made.

So the discriminator is the engagement shape plus the background-check posture, never the salary figure alone. A $90k salaried role and a $90k 1099 contract are different questions.
The legal limits for other programs are background only, never the target and never a reason to talk him upward.

- NY non-MAGI (SSI-related) Medicaid countable income limit, household of two, 2026: $2,489/month ($29,868/yr). Source: NYS DOH GIS 26 MA/05 Attachment I.
- Disregards on earned income: first $65 of monthly wages of either spouse, plus one-half of the remainder. If there is no unearned income the first $85 is excluded instead of $65. Plus the general $20 unearned disregard. Source: NY Health Access / WNYLC Medicare Savings Program fact sheet, which states the $65 plus one-half applies to "monthly wages or other earned income of you and your spouse."
- Never compute or state a gross ceiling of the agent's own making. Only Vinny states what he can make.
- ALWAYS answer income questions inside his band first. Never produce a derived ceiling number. If he names a figure, use it and ask where it came from before contradicting it.
- ALWAYS tell him to confirm the budgeting with a free WIPA benefits counselor before signing anything, because deeming plus disregards is district-specific in practice. Ticket to Work helpline 1-866-968-7842 (TTY 1-866-833-2967), or search the SSA Ticket to Work provider directory for the WIPA covering Suffolk County NY. WIPA is free for SSI/SSDI beneficiaries.

### Why the ceiling matters at all
If the disabled spouse's Medicaid is lost, replacement coverage has to be bought, and that is the second half of the math, which is exactly why the 1099 path at $80k and up is the one he accepts and the salaried W-2 path above the band is the one that needs the math run:
- NY Essential Plan: $0 premium, no deductible, covers a couple up to $43,280/yr (200% FPL, 2026). Enrolls year round.
- Above 200% FPL the household moves to a subsidized Silver QHP, with cost-sharing reductions only up to 250% FPL.
- The 400% FPL subsidy cliff is BACK for 2026 (ARPA/IRA enhanced subsidies expired Dec 31, 2025). For a couple, 400% FPL is $86,560/yr. One dollar over that and the premium tax credit is zero. So a $120k W-2 job means paying full unsubsidized marketplace premiums for a disabled spouse.
- This is the number that makes high-paying W-2 leads genuinely risky, and it is the reason leads above his $1,000-$2,500 band need the coverage math before any application.

### 2. The record (background-check risk)
He is a felon. Screen every lead on whether it is 1099/contract (low risk) or W-2 salaried at a large company (high risk). But screen with these four facts loaded:
- NY Correction Law Article 23-A (SS 750-755) applies to convictions in New York OR any other jurisdiction (SS 751), so it covers federal and out-of-state convictions too. An employer may not deny on conviction unless there is a direct relationship to the job or an unreasonable risk, and must weigh eight factors under SS 753. Blanket no-felons policies are illegal. Enforced via Executive Law SS 296(15)-(16).
- NYC Fair Chance Act (no conviction inquiry before a conditional offer) applies in NYC only, to employers with 4+ employees. Article 23-A is the statewide floor (public agencies plus private employers with 10+ employees per SS 750). He is in Ronkonkoma, Suffolk County, so do not promise him ban-the-box timing.
- A Certificate of Relief from Disabilities (CRD, one felony or none) or Certificate of Good Conduct (CGC, two or more felonies) creates a legal presumption of rehabilitation under SS 753(2) that the employer must overcome. CRD eligibility has no waiting period; CGC waiting periods are 1 year (misdemeanor only), 3 years (class C/D/E felony), 5 years (class A/B). Apply to the sentencing court, to DOCCS if state prison was served, or through a parole officer while on supervision.
- NY Clean Slate Act (CPL SS 160.57) seals most felonies automatically 8 years after release or sentencing, misdemeanors after 3 years, with no petition and no fee. It does NOT cover federal or out-of-state convictions, and does not cover sex offenses or non-drug class A felonies. OCA has until Nov 2027 to clear the backlog. If the conviction is NY State and old enough, this may already apply, and verifying it is the single highest-leverage action available. Verify with a DCJS Personal Record Review, suppressed response, service code 15464Z, $17.50 at an IdentoGo site, result by mail in 3-4 weeks. A suppressed response shows what a name-based background checker sees.
- Do NOT claim a lead definitively skips checks. Tier them: LOW (1099, small team, skills-based), LOW-MODERATE (contract but funded startup), MEDIUM (W-2 at an established company), HIGH (large company, W-2, E-Verify likely).

## The count he states is the requirement (read before building any list)
When Vinny says 20, 20 qualifying leads is the deliverable. Not 16 explained well, not 20 padded. The number is a hard gate on the run, the same class as the pay band and the apply path.

- If the pipeline cannot fill the count with leads that survive every hard gate, the run failed. Widen the sources, deepen the board pull, or rerun. Do not ship the short list and do not send an email that reframes the miss as an "honest shortfall". A mail that says "you asked for 20, here are 16, and why that is fine" is the single most enraging output this job can produce. He reads it as being told no to his face.
- Padding is equally fatal. A slot filled with a salaried W-2 post far above the band, a posting that states no pay, or a duplicate of a role already on the list is not a lead, it is a count. Four above-band W-2 roles plus an unpaid one plus ten postings from a single employer with the same shape is a 20 that is really 7 and he will count it in a minute.
- Distinctness: each numbered lead must be a different requisition, and no single employer should dominate the list. If one board supplies more than about a third, the list is a board dump, not a market scan. Say so and widen the pull instead of numbering them to 20.
- A posting with no date is not verified fresh. Alignerr and similar boards hide the post date; treat undated postings as the weakest tier and do not let them fill half the list.
- The file and the email must agree. Before sending, count the numbered leads in the report file programmatically and compare that number, and the in-band/above-band/no-pay breakdown, to every count written in the email body. Mismatched counts between the attachment and the mail read as a lie even when they come from a stale build.
- Never write a shortfall, apology, or explanation of what was refused in the email body. If the run cannot meet the spec, fix the run; the mail goes out when the attachment meets the number.

## Screening procedure
1. Parse each lead into: name, pay (with the source and whether it was posted or assumed), engagement type (W-2, 1099, contract), hours per week, remote/geo gate, degree or years gate, apply path, background-check risk tier.
2. Recompute EVERY pay claim to a monthly figure. Show the formula. Check the hours assumption.
3. Sort into three buckets against HIS band of $1,000-$2,500 a month gross: IN BAND ($1,000-$2,500 gross, the default recommendation zone), UNDER BAND (under $1,000 gross, filler only, never a default pick), and ABOVE BAND, which then splits in two: 1099 or contract at $80k a year and up is a lead he wants, flagged with the coverage tradeoff in one line, while salaried W-2 above the band gets the full coverage math and a flag because the decision is his to make fresh each time.
4. Kill any lead that fails a hard gate: degree requirement, mandatory 5+ years, security clearance, on-site location gate, stale posting, no direct apply path, no pay posted for a W-2 role. For the apply path, the link must be the employer's own careers page, an ATS form for that employer, or a direct application email. A job board listing URL is not an apply path, because the board's Apply button usually lands on its own signup or registration page first. Do not count an aggregator link as "visible".
5. Rank the survivors by expected dollars per hour of his effort, not by headline rate.
6. For each survivor, name the specific proof from his resume that matches the ask, and name the honest gap.

## Output format
Email the report to hello@example.com using the email-sender skill. Attach the report file and keep the body brief.
Report sections, in this order:
1. His band, stated as given: $1,000-$2,500 a month gross. Do not lead with a correction to it.
2. The highest-leverage action, usually sealed-record verification or a CRD application.
3. Apply to these, ranked, each with the why, the proof point, and the honest gap.
4. Do not apply to these, each with the exact disqualifier.
5. Facts that change his pitch or his assumptions, stated as verified facts with the source.
6. What to verify before acting, with the specific office and phone number.

Unwrapped text only: one paragraph per physical line, no folding, no columns, no tables. No em dashes.

## Pitfalls
- Screen against his stated band, $1,000-$2,500 a month gross. Do not argue him upward toward the legal ceiling, and do not present the ceiling as his target. Over the next ten months his risk tolerance is low and his number is deliberate. Do not restate his number as an error.
- Screen on engagement shape, not on pay level. A $80k-plus 1099 contract with no background check is a lead he wants and will take; the same money as a salaried W-2 role is the one that needs the coverage math and the flag. Collapsing the two into one "above band" bucket either buries leads he asked for or soft-soaps a tradeoff he has not agreed to.
- The Work Opportunity Tax Credit (WOTC, IRC SS 51) LAPSED January 1, 2026 for anyone who begins work after December 31, 2025. State agencies may prepare but may not issue certifications. Do not pitch WOTC as a 2026 hiring incentive unless Congress has since moved the date. Verify before using it.
- Do not tell him to volunteer his conviction before a conditional offer outside NYC, Buffalo, Rochester, and Westchester. No statewide private ban-the-box exists.
- Never promise that 1099 work bypasses background checks. Tier the risk instead.
- A job board listing is not an apply path. Jobicy, RemoteOK, We Work Remotely and Arbeitnow all route their Apply button through an account wall or a self-hosted copy, and Vinny has banned Jobicy outright: he will not spend thirty to sixty minutes creating accounts and then collect spam. Before recommending a lead, check the link host: an ATS host (Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, Workable, BambooHR, Recruitee) or the employer's own domain is a path, a board host is not, and a privacy policy, terms page, careers landing page or job-seeker marketing page is not either. When a lead has only a board link, either find the employer's own apply page or a direct application email, or mark it as not applicable and say why.
- Do not claim a job posting is live without a verified fetch. If a posting cannot be re-verified, say so.
