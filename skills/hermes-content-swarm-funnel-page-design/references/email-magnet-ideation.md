# Email Magnet Ideation from Site Content

## Workflow

1. **Browse the full article library** — scroll through all articles, capture titles, summaries, and category tags
2. **Identify recurring themes and knowledge clusters** — group articles by topic (infrastructure, agents, economics, sovereignty, trust, etc.)
3. **For each cluster, ask:**
   - What concrete thing could I package from these articles?
   - Is it scarce (not available elsewhere)?
   - Is it immediately useful on receipt?
   - Does it create an ongoing reason to stay subscribed?
4. **Map each magnet to a format type:**
   - **Cheat sheet / reference card** — one-pager, ultra-scannable
   - **Checklist** — step-by-step, completable outcome
   - **Directory / living reference** — updated periodically, bookmark-worthy
   - **Playbook / guide** — multi-page, how-to depth
   - **Tracker / report** — recurring delivery, builds habit
5. **Write "What you get" + "Why it works"** for each — the why must tie to a specific article or content pattern on the site
6. **Add strategy notes** — which magnets for broadest appeal vs highest-quality leads, delivery rules (instant, no drip), format constraints (one page preferred)

## Magnet Quality Signals

High-converting magnets share these traits:
- **Specific promise** — not "AI newsletter" but "Non-Western AI Model Directory"
- **Instant delivery** — they get it immediately upon email confirmation
- **Reference quality** — they come back to it, share it with teammates
- **Scarce perspective** — the angle isn't available from 50 other blogs
- **One page** — PhantomByte brand is sharp and dense; don't dilute with 20-page PDFs

## PhantomByte-Specific Patterns

The articles.phantom-byte.com content naturally clusters into these magnet categories:
- **Infrastructure deep dives** → cheat sheets, one-pagers
- **Agent engineering** → playbooks, checklists
- **AI economics/labor** → trackers, monthly reports (broaden beyond devs)
- **Sovereignty/open models** → directories, living references
- **Security/trust** → defense guides (fear-of-missing-out converts well)

## Conversion Benchmarks

- Generic newsletter CTA: 1-2%
- Specific lead magnet: 10-25%
- The gap is entirely about perceived specificity and instant value
