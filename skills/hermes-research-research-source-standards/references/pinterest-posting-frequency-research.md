# Pinterest Posting Frequency Research

## What Pinterest's Official Sources Say

### Sources Checked (July 19, 2026)

1. **help.pinterest.com**, Business help center articles on creating content, optimizing profiles, and best practices. None specify a recommended number of pins per day.

2. **business.pinterest.com**, Business landing pages and guides. No posting frequency guidance found.

3. **create.pinterest.com**, Creator hub with "best practices" and "tips" sections. No daily cadence recommendations.

4. **Pinterest Academy** (pinterestacademy.com), Courses cover campaign setup, creative strategy, and measurement. No posting frequency content.

5. **help.pinterest.com/en/guide/all-about-pinterest**, General guide to the platform. Covers what Pinterest is, how to search, how to save pins. No posting frequency advice.

### Result

**Pinterest does not publish a recommended posting frequency.** Their official guidance focuses on:
- Pinning fresh, original content
- Using high-quality images
- Writing keyword-rich descriptions
- Being consistent

The specific daily numbers commonly found on the internet (3-5/day for new accounts, ramping to 10-15/day) are community consensus and third-party tool research (e.g., Tailwind's publishing data), not platform policy.

## Third-Party Data: Tailwind Blog Research (July 2026)

Tailwind (tailwindapp.com) is the largest Pinterest scheduler/analytics platform. Their blog publishes data-backed research. Key findings from their current articles:

### Recommended Cadence

From *"Pinterest for Shopify: 12 Do's and Don'ts That Drive Real Sales"* (March 26, 2026):
- **5-10 fresh pins/day** is what successful Shopify stores publish
- **3 pins/day minimum**, "3 Pins per day is better than dumping 30 Pins once a week"
- Consistency is the key factor: "Pinterest rewards fresh content over time. Rather than publishing a large batch once and disappearing, it's better to add new Pins consistently."
- "Consistency helps Pinterest understand your account and keeps your content circulating."

### Overposting Warning

From *"Valentine's Day Marketing on Pinterest: Strategy, Timing, and Execution"* (January 7, 2026):
- Overposting leads to follower fatigue and reduced engagement
- Pinterest's spam filters flag accounts that post too much too quickly
- Tailwind's **Pin Spacing** feature exists specifically to prevent this, it enforces minimum intervals between similar pins
- "Post with intent, not just frequency."

### The Power-Law Curve

From *"Tailwind Turbo Beta Results"* (February 2, 2026):
- Only 1 in 56 creator pins reaches 10,000+ impressions
- Pinterest follows a power-law curve: most pins perform modestly, a small percentage account for outsized success
- Quality of the individual pin (visuals, keyword targeting, clear intent) determines breakout potential
- Fresh pins get tested with a few hundred impressions; if engagement signals are strong, Pinterest expands distribution
- Turbo Pins (expert-curated) reached 100,000+ impressions 312x more often than average

### Summary Table

| Factor | Recommendation |
|--------|---------------|
| Minimum cadence | 3 fresh pins/day |
| Growth cadence | 5-10 fresh pins/day |
| Spacing | Spread across the day (2-3 hour gaps) |
| Content type | Fresh pins only (new image or URL each time) |
| Quality gate | One high-quality pin that gets saved > 50 low-effort pins |
| Risk zone | Posting too fast triggers spam filters |
| Key metric | Consistency over volume |

## What This Means for Future Queries
