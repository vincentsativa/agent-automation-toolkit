METHODOLOGY
================================================================================
Google Trends analysis conducted [date]. Three batches of up to 5 terms each,
US market, Past 12 months, Web Search. Terms classified into three tiers
based on Trends Average value and competition landscape.

================================================================================
Use sparingly -- primarily as metadata/SEO umbrella terms.

1. data center water usage -- Avg 44
   Trend: Consistently high, peaked Aug 2025 (100). Related rising queries:

   "ai data center water usage" (+80%).

TIER 2: MEDIUM COMPETITION (Avg 10-25)
================================================================================
PRIMARY TARGETS. Optimize title, H1, URL, first 100 words, meta description.

3. data center moratorium -- Avg 18
   Trend: Spiking hard. Exploded to 100 on May 17, 2026 (Seattle moratorium).
   Related: "seattle data center moratorium" (Breakout), "ai data center
   moratorium act" (Breakout).
   CRITICAL WINDOW: Breaking news. 1-2 weeks before major pubs cover it.

4. data center permitting -- Avg 14
   Trend: Rising steadily. Was 0-5 in early 2025, now consistently 15-31.
   No breakout terms yet -- opportunity to own this space.

5. data center opposition -- Avg 10
   Trend: Rising. Was 0-5 in mid-2025, now consistently 10-29.
   Related: "new hampshire data center opposition" (Breakout).
   USE: Title, H1, URL candidate.

6. data center protests -- Avg 10
   Trend: Rising, event-driven. Peaked Jan 2026 at 26.
