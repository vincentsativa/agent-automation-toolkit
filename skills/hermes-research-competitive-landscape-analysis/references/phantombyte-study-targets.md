# PhantomByte Study Targets (May 2026)

Context: Vinny Barreca, two months into daily publishing, running three properties (articles.phantom-byte.com, phantom-byte.com, vincentsativa.com). Needs to study operators, not giants.

---

## Content Strategy

### Simon Willison
- URL: https://simonwillison.net
- Study: Link-blog cadence, TIL posts as low-effort SEO entries, title structure for search intent
- Key insight: Daily publishing doesn't require daily 3,000-word essays. One sharp observation + generous outlinks compounds.

### Lilian Weng
- URL: https://lilianweng.github.io
- Study: Depth-to-frequency ratio. Infrequent but canonical posts that rank for years.
- Key insight: Topic selection matters more than frequency. Pick questions that will still matter in 2030.

### Dan Luu
- URL: https://danluu.com
- Study: Anti-SEO approach. Zero design, massive authority. Empirical rigor spreads via word of mouth.
- Key insight: Substance over presentation for an engineering audience.

## Monetization

### Pieter Levels
- URL: https://levels.io
- Study: Building-in-public funnel. Twitter → blog → product pipeline. Multiple AI products as one person.
- Key insight: Audience trust converts faster than ad spend.

### Marc Lou
- URL: https://marclou.com
- Study: Speed-to-revenue. Idea validation via tweet before code. SMB AI tools market.
- Key insight: The AI tools market for SMBs is winnable by individuals.

## Audience Building

### Lenny Rachitsky
- URL: https://lennysnewsletter.com
- Study: Growth tactics. Guest cross-posts, podcast-to-newsletter pipeline, converting personal network into first subscribers.
- Key insight: Growth playbook applies across niches.

### Swyx
- URL: https://swyx.io, Latent Space podcast
- Study: "Learn in Public" as audience engine. Turning content authority into media company.
- Key insight: Community around AI engineering before it was a category.

## Services Model

### Brett from DesignJoy
- URL: https://designjoy.co
- Study: Productized subscription services. Flat monthly fee, Trello board, async delivery. $40k+/month, one person.
- Key insight: Operational template for selling technical services without becoming an agency.
