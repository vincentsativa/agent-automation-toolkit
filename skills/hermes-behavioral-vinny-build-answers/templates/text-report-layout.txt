Skeleton for a text report delivered to Vinny. Copy this and fill it in; do not
reflow it into paragraphs. Every body paragraph stays on ONE unwrapped line, and
the visual break comes from the rulers, the ALL-CAPS headings, the indented
blocks, and the blank lines.

================================================================
TITLE IN CAPS, NAMING THE ACTION AND ITS OUTPUT
One line saying what the document contains. Date.
================================================================

WHAT THIS IS
One or two lines of framing, then get out of the way. No apology block, no
history of earlier drafts.


================================================================
ONE. FIRST ITEM, NAMED AS AN ACTION
================================================================

    "The instruction they can paste, in quotation marks, naming inputs,

WHAT IT READS

WHAT COMES BACK

Layout rules that make the difference between readable and a wall:
- Ruler lines of 64 characters before and after every section.
- One blank line minimum between every block; two before a new section.
- Instruction text indented four spaces, quoted, never merged into a paragraph.
- Bullets only for received output fields, never for prose.
- No column wrapping of paragraphs, ever.
- Max line length check before sending: long lines are correct, short uniform
  lines mean the text was wrapped and the file is wrong.
