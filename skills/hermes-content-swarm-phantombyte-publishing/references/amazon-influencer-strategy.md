# Amazon Influencer Storefront Strategy

## Context

Vinny was accepted into the Amazon Influencer program (late June 2026). His storefront is at `amazon.com/shop/influencer-1c633519`. Bio: "I build AI tools, bots & lead-gen weapons for individuals & small businesses. Code from the Shadows."

Current state: blank, "No posts yet." He has started monetization for his Pinterest pins and now has free time to work on this.

## Why It Matters

- Amazon Influencer pages get indexed by Google and can rank for long-tail product queries
- Amazon.com carries high domain authority, posts here can outrank smaller sites
- Direct monetization channel (commissions on every click)
- Cross-pollination with PhantomByte, every product recommended in an article can be mirrored here as a shoppable post
- Amazon's algorithm favors active creators with content, especially video

## Content Types That Work

1. **Product roundups ("Best of" lists)**, AI coding assistants, VPNs for developers, noise-cancelling headphones for deep work
2. **Tech & gear Vinny actually uses**, monitor, keyboard, mouse, mic, webcam, standing desk, USB hubs, NAS drives
3. **Books**, AI/ML books, security books, programming books, indie hacker reads
4. **Software subscriptions**, Claude Pro, ChatGPT Plus, GitHub Copilot, AWS credits (sold via Amazon gift cards/subscriptions)
5. **Short video reviews (30-60 seconds)**, unboxings, "why I use this" clips. Amazon heavily favors video in search. Videos get placed on actual product pages, not just the storefront.
6. **Seasonal/deal posts**, Prime Day tech picks, Black Friday dev tool deals
