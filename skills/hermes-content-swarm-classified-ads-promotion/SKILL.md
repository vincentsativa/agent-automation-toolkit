# Classified Ads Promotion

## Procedure
1. Fetch the platform's terms/rules page with scrapling BEFORE drafting. Extract the user-conduct and posting rules (spam, duplicate posts, wrong category, multiple accounts, contests). Complying beats getting flagged and re-doing the ad.
2. Write the ad to a text file in the user's home directory (e.g. `[site]_[service]_ad.txt`) so he can edit before posting. He posts himself; never auto-post.
3. Email the file via the email-sender skill (hello@example.com). Include in the email: the ad, any account-setup reminder, and a rules-compliance note mapping each relevant rule to how the ad complies. Sign "Sent by Karma (Vinny's Hermes agent)."
4. Suggest other posting venues, ranked by buyer fit, not just generic classifieds.

## Ad style (Vinny's preference)
- Socratic method: a chain of pointed yes/no questions exposing the pain, then the offer, ending with a free-audit or low-commitment hook and a reply trigger phrase.
- No em dashes. Plain text, paste-ready, title + body clearly labeled.

## Channel guidance
- General classifieds (Oodle, Craigslist) are lottery tickets: near-zero for technical B2B services. Post once, move on, do not invest time.
- Higher-fit channels for technical/SEO services: Reddit niche subs, Facebook groups for the buyer's platform, BlackHatWorld/DigitalPoint/Warrior Forum services sections (buyers with wallets, can post openly with price), Alignable, Nextdoor, LinkedIn groups.
- For lead-gen, prefer a free-audit lead-magnet page on his site (with schema markup for GEO) as the destination for all channels; fixed-price packages convert better than hourly for small businesses.
- Paid ads stay off until the September revenue window per his business plan.

## Pitfalls
- Terms pages are often not at /terms/; enumerate footer links (css 'a') and follow the info/terms path.
- One account, one post, real category/city/reply address on every platform that checks for duplicates or astroturfing.
