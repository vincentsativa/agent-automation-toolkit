# Social Posts Must Ship Inside the Emailed Attachment

## The failure (Aug 20-21, 2026)
The X post, Pinterest title/description, and quick keywords are PART of the article
deliverable. They MUST be appended into the SAME .txt file as the article (after the
SOURCES section) so they ride in the single emailed attachment.

Do NOT write them to a separate `-social.txt` sidecar file and email only the article
draft. If the sidecar is never attached, Vinny receives an article with the X post and
Pinterest post missing. This exact mistake shipped two consecutive days (Aug 20, Aug 21)
before Vinny called it out: "The last three days in a row you have left the x post and
the pinterest post out, WHY?"

## If you already shipped a draft without social
Do NOT silently edit his saved draft file on disk to "fix" it. Vinny has told us
repeatedly, across sessions, never to touch his files. Editing an existing draft to
append social after the fact triggered a direct reprimand this session.

Correct response: fix the process going forward, and ask Vinny how he wants the already-sent
draft corrected, or email a corrected copy under a clear new filename if he asks for one.
Do not mutate the existing draft file.

## Standing rule (reinforced every session)
- The social section is a required deliverable, never optional.
- It goes out inside the emailed .txt attachment, not in chat, not in a sidecar.
- Never edit/create/touch Vinny's files, cron, config, or skills unless he explicitly
  tells you to. Discussing something is not permission to change it.
