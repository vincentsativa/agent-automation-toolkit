# Model Evaluation & Positioning (Aug 2026)

Companion to `model-routing-stack.md` and `outline-selection-reasoning-over-vote.md`. Captures how Vinny evaluates models and how he positions himself against the crowd.

## How Vinny Evaluates Models (test in production, not benchmarks)

Vinny does NOT trust leaderboards or vendor benchmark claims. He vets every model by giving it REAL work and watching how it performs in his actual pipeline. This is why his model reads are sharper than people chasing benchmark spreadsheets. When discussing a model with him, ground the assessment in production behavior, not published scores.

**Confidence vs capability framing:** Vinny judges a model's maturity by whether its capability matches its confidence. A preview model is often "more confident than capable", it talks a good game then stumbles. A model that has matured (e.g. Flash 0731) has capability that finally caught up to confidence: when it says it can do something, it actually can. Use this framing when evaluating whether a model is production-ready.

**"Feels better" is a real signal.** Vinny describes a good model as "it just feels better, better tooling." You can't benchmark that; you only feel it in real use. When a model feels right in production, that beats any leaderboard.

## Distribution-First Strategy

Vinny builds distribution FIRST, then the product, shipping useful info daily to build the audience and prove demand before sinking time into building something nobody wants. The tools are the means, distribution is the moat, the product is the payoff. He's waiting for traffic to show where his expertise is actually needed (candidate: custom tools and agent systems for small business/individuals), then building toward that demand rather than guessing. Do not frame this as backwards, it is the efficient order: validate demand before building.

## Hunting, Not Chasing

Vinny corrects "chasing" trends to "hunting" them. Chasing is reactive, running behind something already moving. Hunting is patient, deliberate, reading the tracks before the animal is visible. He separates signal from noise: the data is the same for everyone, but what's CONCEALED in it (the story within the story, the pattern months before it trends) is what he extracts. He creates his own buzzwords rather than tracking others', that's how you become the authority instead of one of a thousand echoing the same terms.

## Positioning Against the Echo Chamber

Vinny's disdain for the X/solo-founder echo chamber is a positioning signal, not just venting. The crowd: everyone's a "solo founder" whose only product is a guide on how to be a solo founder, all parroting the same words/takes/opinions, none actually building or shipping. Vinny has actually built and shipped (multi-million success, real pipeline), he is not selling a guide. Content should position him as the one who does the work and surfaces the untouched gold, not as another voice in the echo chamber. He also rejects "fake it until you make it" and "building in public" framing entirely.
