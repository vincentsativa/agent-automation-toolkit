# Outline Selection: Reasoning Over Vote (Aug 2026)

## The Panel Votes, But Reasoning Decides

Vinny's outline selection committee (GPT Luna, Grok 4.6, Gemini 4.7, Gemma 4, DeepSeek Flash) VOTES on candidate outlines — but the vote is NOT the determining factor. What matters is the REASONING behind each vote. Each model gives a different angle on why an outline is good:

- One may favor a story that gets views NOW (short-term reach).
- Another may favor a more EVERGREEN piece (long-term compounding authority).

When the panel splits (each picks a different outline), the reasoning is the only thing that breaks the tie. Vinny weighs the reasons and makes the final call himself, often based on positioning — how he wants to be seen that day. This is a BRAND/positioning decision, not a pure quality call.

## Implication for the Agent

When Vinny asks which outline to write, surface the strategic tradeoffs (views-now vs evergreen, authority vs reach) rather than just ranking on fixed criteria. The decision is positioning-driven, not quality-driven. All the outlines are usually strong ("all fire") — the bottleneck is which direction fits the day, not which is best.

## The Data Moat (why the outlines are all strong)

Vinny's edge is superior data scraping — real, current signal, not recycled garbage. He hunts patterns CONCEALED in the data and connects dots months before trends surface. He does NOT chase keywords/trends (that's what "the idiots" do — they don't scrape, they just prompt "write a viral article based on today's trending news"). He creates his own buzzwords rather than tracking others'. The models are good, but the data pipeline is the moat that separates him from the pack. This is how he becomes the authority: writing what's already written is boring and crowded; surfacing the untouched gold is the moat.
