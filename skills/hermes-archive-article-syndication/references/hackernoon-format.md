# HackerNoon Submission Format

## Story Settings fields (all set before submitting, none optional)

- **Title:** 60 to 100 characters is the optimum band (16 to 18 words). No links in the title, no link in the subtitle. Title Case unless there is a stated reason.
- **Meta description:** 120 to 160 characters. Shorter is rejected as missing.
- **Tags:** exactly 8, 30 characters maximum each. Order drives curation and distribution: tag one is what appears beside the story on tagged pages, and tags one and two become the Twitter hashtags. Put the most searched, most on-point tag first.
- **First seen at:** the canonical field. Fill it only when the same text already exists on the writer's own site in good standing. Leave it empty when the platform copy is the first publication.
- **Featured image:** required in practice. No brand logo or brand depiction in it, and credit the source in the caption as `(Source: Name)`.
- **Vested interest:** disclose at the TOP and the BOTTOM of the story in italics when the writer is linked to any product, service, or company covered. The platform's own wording: a linked writer who states it in the bio is not treated as paid promotion, but the disclosure is still what the editors look for.
- **TL;DR:** an editor-only field, 1,000 characters maximum, sitting directly under the title. The body's first paragraph is the abstract, and the TL;DR is the compressed version of it. Fill the field on every draft; it is an editor-side control, never a body section.

## Rejection reasons worth pre-checking

- Paid promotion, or vested interest not disclosed.
- Not tech-related at all. The thesis has to connect back to technology somewhere.
- Backlink misuse. At most 1 promotional link per 500 words, and links to other reputable sources must outnumber brand links (roughly 2 or 3 to 1). No UTM links unless the writer is a partner.
- Unattributed previously published content. Treated as plagiarism if the First seen at field is empty and the piece already ran elsewhere.
- Weak conclusion, or a bio or company profile tacked on at the end. Close with insight and a clear next action, not a bio.
- No images at all.
- Headline that is misleading, plagiarized, or clickbait.

## Submission mechanics

- Editor: `https://app.hackernoon.com/new`. Import an existing post by direct URL or RSS, or write in the editor directly.
- Story Settings sits in the upper right of the draft editor. Submit for review from there.
- Review takes about 3 to 4 business days at the median, not immediate.
- Hand Vinny the finished file; he submits it. Do not publish or submit on his behalf.

## Pre-send verification for a HackerNoon draft

Run this list, not from memory, over the finished file:

1. Title length inside 60 to 100 characters, no links inside it.
2. Meta description inside 120 to 160 characters.
3. Exactly 8 tags, each 30 characters or fewer, first tag on-point.
4. Zero em dashes, en dashes, and spaced hyphens in the body.
5. Every number traceable to the artifact the post is built on, and every code or command block that matters present and correctly copied.
6. Any absolute claim about a mechanism, a count, or a platform verified before shipping. A plausible sounding sentence that is slightly wrong is the one a technical reviewer catches, and it discredits the numbers around it.
7. Time phrases match the artifact. A scan run days ago is not "two weeks ago."
8. Vested interest disclosed up top and at the bottom when the writer sells anything described.
9. Featured image decision stated, with no logo in it.
