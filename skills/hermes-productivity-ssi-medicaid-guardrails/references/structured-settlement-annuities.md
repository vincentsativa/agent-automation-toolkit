# Structured-settlement annuity tier investigation

Use when a disabled beneficiary's annuity may have multiple payout tiers, a contested end date, or a lifetime-vs-term contradiction.

## The core contradiction signal
"Pays for life" and "ends on a specific date" cannot both describe one policy:
- A **lifetime annuity** pays until death; it has NO termination date.
- A **term-certain annuity** (e.g. a 10/15/20-year period) pays a fixed amount for a fixed period, then stops.
Quick math sanity check: principal / monthly payment ≈ number of months it can possibly run. E.g. $863k / $4k/mo ≈ 18 years → that is a term-certain design, not life-long.

## The two explanations
1. **Always term-certain, "for life" was family/lawyer shorthand.** No recourse; you now just know the real end date and can plan around it.
2. **The lifetime payments were sold/factored** — owner traded remaining lifetime payments for a cash lump (JG Wentworth is the well-known factoring archetype), converting them into a fixed-term payout that ends on a date. Under NY's structured-settlement transfer law this REQUIRES court approval → there is a public court file and possible recourse. Get the purchase/transfer agreement and the court order.

## Multi-tier structure (the common false-alarm)
Structured settlements often build payments in tiers: a **period-certain phase** paying X for N years, then rolling into a **lifetime annuity phase** with no end date. In that case both statements the family were told can be true at once: the date is the end of tier one, not the end of all payment.

### Why phone reps mislead (innocently)
Line-level servicing reps often see ONLY the current active payout phase. Later tiers are not on their screen — they auto-roll in when the current phase ends. A rep will confidently quote "it ends <date>" from tier one. They are not lying; they are reading the current tier.

### Ask-for-it-in-writing script
> "Does a lifetime annuity payment tier begin after the period-certain tier that ends <date> — and if so, what is the monthly amount and the start date? Please provide the full payment schedule including all riders."

Push past the phone rep if needed (servicing department), and always request the complete multi-tier schedule IN WRITING. Also request the original settlement payout/distribution schedule — the annuity mirrors it and shows what tiers were supposed to exist.

## Later tiers usually pay LESS
Early tiers are front-loaded; a lifetime tier commonly pays below the period-certain tier. The decision-critical number is the lifetime tier's monthly amount, not "does it continue." Get dollar amounts per phase.

## Order of operations
1. Get the annuity contract itself — read the TERM language ("for life" / "term certain" / number of years).
2. Get any purchase/transfer/factoring agreement + court order — public and searchable if a sale happened.
3. One consult with a structured-settlement attorney (NY) showing both — recoverable money only if a life annuity was sold under her; otherwise you gain certainty to plan on.
4. Verify the written contract over any verbal/hand-me-down account. The document is the only ground truth.
