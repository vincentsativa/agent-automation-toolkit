# PhantomByte Craigslist AI Services Strategy Skill

## When to Use
- Vinny asks to research Craigslist for PhantomByte services/offers
- Vinny asks for a Craigslist posting strategy, lead generation plan, or service listing research
- Any request to find services to promote on Craigslist using open-source AI tools

## Output Target
- **To:** hello@phantom-byte.com
- **Subject:** "PhantomByte Craigslist AI Services Strategy Guide" or context-specific
- **Attachment:** A .txt document with the full strategy

## Execution Steps

### 1. Scout Craigslist Categories
Browse the target categories in 2–3 major US cities to understand the competitive landscape:
- **Computer Services (cps):** Tech-specific posts. Best for AI/tech services.
- **Small Biz Ads (biz):** Broader business owner reach. More volume, more noise.

**Cities to scout:**
- Tier 1 (high volume, competitive): Los Angeles, New York City, San Francisco, Chicago
- Tier 2 (growing, less saturated): Seattle, Austin, Denver, Miami, Phoenix, Dallas

**What to capture:**
- Total listing count for the category
- The top 10–15 post titles (note the spam patterns)
- Any post that mentions "AI" (note how vague it is)
- The "SEE ALSO" sidebar links (SEO, web design, tech support, etc.)

**Red-flag spam patterns to document:**
- ALL CAPS titles with excessive emojis (🔥🚀⭐)
- Vague hype: "Power up your business with AI" with no deliverable
- No price listed ("Call for quote")
- No tool named
- Misspelled or generic copy-paste text

### 2. Identify the Gap
Write a 1-paragraph competitive analysis:
- What is EVERY existing post doing wrong?
- What is NO ONE offering?
- Where is the trust gap? (Named tools, fixed prices, specific outcomes)

### 3. Develop 10 Legitimate Open-Source AI Services
Each service must be:
- Built on free/open-source tools (no SaaS lock-in)
- Priced as one-time setup + optional maintenance
- Deliverable in 48–72 hours
- Explainable in plain English
- Differentiated from Craigslist spam

**For each of the 10 services, document:**

**A. Craigslist Title (exact)**
- Outcome-focused format: "[Specific AI Outcome] — $[Price] Setup"
- Pain-focused format: "Stop [Pain Point] — [AI Solution]"
- Comparison-focused format: "Ditch [SaaS] — Self-Hosted [Alternative]"

**B. What It Is (2–3 sentences)**
- The specific business problem solved
- The tangible outcome the client gets

**C. Open-Source Stack**
- Name each tool (Ollama, n8n, Botpress, Cal.com, Odoo, Whisper, etc.)
- Note what each layer does (LLM, automation, hosting, UI)

**D. Target Business Type**
- 5–8 specific industries that feel this pain most acutely

**E. Pricing**
- One-time setup fee
- Monthly maintenance fee (optional)
- Add-on pricing (clearly defined)

**F. Best 3 Cities/States**
- For each city, write 3–4 sentences explaining WHY this city wins:
  1. Market density (industry concentration)
  2. Craigslist saturation (low quality tech posts = easy to stand out)
  3. Business culture (tech-savvy? cost-conscious? franchise-heavy?)
  4. Unique geographic/demographic edge (bilingual, tourism, seasonal, etc.)

## Pitfalls
- **Never skip the Craigslist scouting step.** City/category volumes change. What was true last month may not be true now. Always browse current listings.
- **Never post in "gigs."** That category is for one-off labor. PhantomByte services belong in "services" (computer services or small biz ads) to attract ongoing client relationships.
- **Never use the same title twice in 14 days in the same city.** Craigslist auto-flags duplicates. Always rotate titles.
- **Never promise regulatory outcomes.** Contract review is "a second pair of eyes," not legal advice. Medical automation is workflow, not diagnosis.
- **Never offer AI image generation or generic AI marketing.** These are saturated, low-perceived-value, and attract tire-kickers.
- **Never skip the revenue projection.** Vinny needs the business model math in every strategy doc.
